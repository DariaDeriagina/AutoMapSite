The people behind the service

OUR TEAM

Rami

Head Mechanic

Rami leads the team with a Master’s degree in mechanical engineering and more than 30 years of experience in automotive repair. His extensive background includes 17 years as a trade instructor in technical colleges and 3 years as a head technician at Jordan Speed Centre, specializing in performance vehicles, drift, and rally cars. Combining technical mastery with leadership, Rami ensures that every repair and diagnostic procedure meets premium service expectations.

Mohammad

Lead Mechanic & Automotive Electrician

Mohammad is a highly qualified specialist with a Bachelor’s degree in electrical technology and Electromechanics engineering and has over seven years of experience in servicing hybrid and gas vehicles. His expertise covers complete vehicle maintenance, engine and transmission rebuilding, suspension system repairs, advanced electrical diagnostics, and programming of car modules. Known for precision and innovation, Mohammad ensures each vehicle is serviced to the highest industry standards.

What We Do

SERVICES

Preventative Maintenance & Diagnostics

Regular inspections and tune-ups to catch issues early and keep your car running smoothly.

Engine & Transmission Rebuild and Replacement

Full repair or replacement of your vehicle's engine or transmission to restore peak performance.

Suspension Repairing

Fixing shocks, struts, and other suspension parts for a safer, smoother ride.

Oil Change

Quick oil and filter changes to keep your engine lubricated and extend its life.

ECU Tuning

Custom software adjustments to your car's computer for better power, fuel efficiency, and performance.

Electrical Wiring Problem Solving

Diagnosing and repairing electrical issues like faulty lights, sensors, or wiring.

Safety Check

Comprehensive inspection to ensure your vehicle meets safety standards and is road-ready.

Body Work and Welding

Cosmetic and structural repairs from dents to frame welding — we restore your car's look and integrity.

Exhaust Tuning

Enhancing your exhaust system for better sound, efficiency, and engine breathing.
